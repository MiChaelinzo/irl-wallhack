About these readings

What WiFi sensing can estimate

  • Presence and motion, from changes in signal strength.
  • Breathing rate of a still person, when readings arrive at 4 per second or faster.
  • Heart rate and activity level, only with high-rate CSI (8 Hz or more). Low confidence.

What it cannot do

  • Mood or emotion: there is no established way to infer it from WiFi. Not shown.
  • Perspiration: no WiFi or listed-sensor method. Not shown.
  • Images, or seeing through walls: signals give coarse activity, never a picture.

Air quality values come only from optional physical sensors, or are labelled SIMULATED. See docs/hardware.md in the repository for supported hardware.
